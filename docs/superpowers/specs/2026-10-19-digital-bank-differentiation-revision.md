## Claim and feature boundaries

Use current official documentation and release metadata, not the installed skills' historical maturity labels. Workflows is documented GA from Stack9.4; particular integrations/steps can retain separate maturity and connector/permission requirements. Streams core/classic management and wired/AI capabilities must be distinguished. AI assistance needs a configured approved model connection; no claim that on-premises storage establishes local inference. Verify exact release and entitlement; describe preview functionality explicitly when shown.

Show Streams as a guided curation and log-management experience, with processing preview and approved changes. Do not say all sources are automatically structured or that no setup/governance is required. Partitioning/wired or advanced capabilities need their own maturity qualification. No live product UI imitation or claims of measured pipeline savings.

Workflows examples are illustrative authored runbooks, not actual executions. Native Elastic queries/actions and configured external ticketing/notification/HTTP integrations must be distinguished. Do not invent a native approval screen or claim every bank action exists out of the box. Use the bank's existing approval process or an explicitly configured integration. Do not trigger customer-impacting changes automatically in the example. Execution history is not a legal compliance guarantee.

Customer outcomes predate the illustrated Streams/Workflows examples. Never attribute those outcomes to either feature. Oney measures detection; WePay measures time to identify impact in Google Cloud, with APM described as a future plan; Emirates NBD provides on-premises logs/APM evidence, not Saudi hosting or regulatory approval. Retain concise attribution and measure labels on slides; deeper qualifications in notes.

For the shortened fictional RCA/payment example, keep candidate cause distinct from verified cause and API success distinct from payment completion. External providers remain unobserved unless instrumented. Public source deck, private FSI attachment and old screenshots remain excluded from history and downloads. Use new synthetic fields only, with no customer identifiers.
